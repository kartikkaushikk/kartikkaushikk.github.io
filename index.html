Chalk & Sum

A calm number puzzle. Arrange the numbers, pick the operators, and reach the target on a chalkboard.

Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Email kartik14704@gmail.com. It helps to mention your device, your iOS version and the level you were on.

Common questions

How do I move my progress to a new phone?
Leave iCloud sync on in Settings and sign in with the same Apple ID on the new device.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads.
Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases.

How do I get more hints?
You get 3 free hints every day, one more for finding every way on a level, and you can watch an optional ad for another.
